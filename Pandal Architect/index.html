Blueprint Manual & Rules

Core Objectives

As the Lead Pandal Architect, assemble the sacred festival enclosure before time expires while keeping costs under your designated budget.

Essential Parameters

  • Stability: Must reach the level target. If stability drops to 0 or below, the pandal collapses immediately!
  • Decoration: Reflects visual grandeur and festival spirit.
  • Eco Value: High eco-friendly materials award a +10% bonus score. Excess plastic banners cause score penalties!
  • Crowd Safety: In high-traffic stages, install illuminated Exit Signs to protect the crowd.

Environmental Hazards

  • Monsoon Rain: Degrades stability continuously unless protective Canopies are erected.
  • Wind Gusts: Storm gusts damage stability unless you anchor with at least 4 sturdy pillars.
  • Crowd Surges: Overcrowding damages decoration unless at least 2 Exit Signs are present.

Controls & Shortcuts

  • 1–9: Quick-select item from toolbar
  • Left Click: Place selected item on grid
  • Right Click / Shift + Click: Dismantle element (50% cost refund)
  • Space: Place item on currently hovered cell
  • F: Submit and finish level
  • Escape: Pause game
